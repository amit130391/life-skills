# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## Question:
1. What kinds of behavior cause sexual harassment?

## Answer:
Some of the kinds of behavior causing sexual harassment are:
- Making unwanted physical contact, such as touching or hugging someone without their consent.
- Making inappropriate sexual jokes, comments, or remarks that make others feel uncomfortable.
- Commenting disrespectfully on someone's body, appearance, or personal life.
- Repeatedly asking someone for dates or sexual favours even after they have said no.
- Sending unwanted sexual messages, pictures, or videos through social media or other platforms.
- Misusing power or authority to demand sexual favours in exchange for promotions or job benefits.
- Threatening, pressuring, or intimidating someone for refusing unwanted sexual advances.

## Question:
2. What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

## Answer:
- I would remain calm and firmly tell the person that their behaviour is inappropriate and unacceptable.
- Support the victim and make them feel safe and comfortable.
- Report the incident to my manager, HR department, or the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC).
- Keep a record of the incidents, including dates, times, messages, and names of witnesses.
- I would not ignore or encourage such behaviour and would stand against it.
- Maintain confidentiality and cooperate with the investigation to ensure appropriate action is taken.

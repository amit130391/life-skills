# Learning Process

## 1. How to Learn Faster with the Feynman Technique

### Question 1
What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

### Answer
The Feynman Technique is a method for learning anything faster. Whenever you learn a new concept, try explaining it to someone as if you were teaching a kid. This helps you identify the areas where your understanding is weak or where you are still unsure.

### Question 3
What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

### Answer
* Active Mode: This is the state where you turn your full attention to a specific task. It involves thinking tightly about concepts you are already somewhat familiar with, like historical patterns or mathematical tables. It works best for processing familiar information in a sequential, logical way.
* Diffused Mode: This is a relaxed set of neural states. When you are in this mode, your thoughts can range very widely, allowing you to make broad, creative connections. This mode is essential when you are trying to grapple with new concepts or techniques that you have never encountered before, as it helps you step away from the "rubber bumpers" of familiar patterns and find new perspectives.

## 4. Learn Anything in 20 hours

### Question 4
According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.

### Answer
According to the video the four steps to learn any new skill are:
* **Deconstruct the skill:** Breaking down the skill into more manageable parts and identify the most important component to practice first.
* **Learn enough to self-correct:** Gather just enough resources like books, DVDs, etc. to understand the basics so you can practice and notice your own mistakes.
* **Remove barriers to practice:** Eliminate distractions like television or the internet to make it easier to sit down and focus on your practice.
* **Practice for at least 20 hours:** Pre-commit to the practice to overcome the initial "frustration barrier" and gain proficiency.

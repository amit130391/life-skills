# Learning Process

## 1. How to Learn Faster with the Feynman Technique

### Question 1
What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

### Answer
Feynman Technique is related to learning anything faster. If you learn anything such as a new concept then explain it to someone. Explain it like you are teaching it to a kid. In this way you would understand where you are lacking or in which areas you are shaky.

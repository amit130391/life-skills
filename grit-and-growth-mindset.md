# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit

#### Question 1
Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.

#### Answer 1
Angela Duckworth explains that grit, a combination of passion and perseverance, is more important for success than IQ or talent. By staying focused on long-term goals and believing that abilities can improve through effort, we can achieve success.

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset

#### Question 2
Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.

#### Answer 2
The video explains that a fixed mindset believes abilities cannot change, while a growth mindset believes they can improve through effort and practice. A growth mindset helps us embrace challenges, learn from mistakes, accept feedback, and keep improving.

## 3. Understanding Internal Locus of Control

#### Question 3
What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

#### Answer 3
Internal Locus of Control is the belief that our success or failure depends mainly on our own actions, efforts, and decisions, rather than luck or external circumstances. The key point is that taking responsibility for our actions helps us stay motivated and persistent. Instead of blaming others or circumstances, we should focus on what we can control and improve through our own efforts.

## 4. How to build a Growth Mindset

#### Question 4
What are the key points mentioned by speaker to build growth mindset

#### Answer 4
According to Brendon Burchard, there are four key points to develop a growth mindset:
1. Believe in Your Ability: Have confidence that you can solve problems, learn new skills, and improve yourself.
2. Question Your Assumptions: Don't let your fears, current limitations, or labels define your future potential.
3. Create Your Own Learning Plan: Actively seek knowledge, read books, learn new skills, and take steps toward your goals.
4. Embrace the Struggle: Accept failures and difficulties as part of the learning process instead of giving up.

# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit

#### Question 1
Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.

#### Answer 1
Angela Duckworth explains that grit, a combination of passion and perseverance, is more important for success than IQ or talent. By staying focused on long-term goals and believing that abilities can improve through effort, we can achieve success.

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset

#### Question 2
Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.

#### Answer 2
The video explains that a fixed mindset believes abilities cannot change, while a growth mindset believes they can improve through effort and practice. A growth mindset helps us embrace challenges, learn from mistakes, accept feedback, and keep improving.

# Listening and Active Communication

## 1. Active Listening

#### Question 1
What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

#### Answer
Some of the steps/strategies to do Active Listening are:
1. Pay full attention to the speaker and avoid getting distracted by your own thoughts or other things around you.
2. Allow the other person to finish speaking completely before you respond or share your thoughts.
3. Use encouraging words and phrases like “I see” or “Tell me more” to show interest and encourage the speaker to continue.
4. Show that you are interested in the conversation through eye contact, nodding, and appropriate body language.
5. Write down important points during important conversations so that you can remember the information later.
6. Repeat or summarize what the speaker has said in your own words to make sure you have understood the message correctly.

## 2. Reflective Listening

#### Question 2
According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

#### Answer
According to Fisher's model, the key points of Reflective Listening are:
- Listen more and speak less, giving the speaker enough time to express their thoughts and feelings.
- Try to understand the speaker's feelings and personal experiences instead of focusing only on facts or general information.
- Repeat or explain what the speaker has said in your own words to show that you have understood their message.
- Avoid asking too many questions, giving advice, or telling the speaker what they should think or feel.
- Try to understand the situation from the speaker's point of view without judging them or comparing it with your own experiences.
- Show empathy, acceptance, and genuine interest so that the speaker feels comfortable sharing their thoughts and feelings.

## 3. Reflection

#### Question 3
What are the obstacles in your listening process?

#### Answer
The obstacles in my listening process are:

- Sometimes I get distracted by my own thoughts while someone is speaking.
- I may lose focus when the speaker talks for a long time.
- I sometimes think about my response before the speaker has finished talking.
- Sometimes I find it difficult to understand the speaker's feelings and point of view.
- I may make assumptions about what the speaker is going to say instead of listening carefully.

#### Question 4
What can you do to improve your listening?

#### Answer
- I can improve my listening by giving full attention to the speaker and avoiding distractions.
- I can be patient and focus on understanding the speaker's message.
- Maintain eye contact and use positive body language to show interest.
- I can avoid thinking about my response while the other person is speaking.
- I can ask relevant questions to clarify anything I do not understand.
- I can summarize the speaker's words in my own language to ensure proper understanding.
- I can practice active listening in my daily conversations to become a better listener.

## 4. Types of Communication

#### Question 5
When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day to day life?

#### Answer
- I switch to a passive communication style when I want to avoid arguments or conflicts with others.
- I use this style when talking to elders or people in authority to show respect.
- Sometimes, I stay quiet when I am afraid of hurting someone's feelings.
- I may avoid expressing my opinions when I feel that others might disagree with me.
- I use passive communication when I want to maintain peace and harmony in relationships.
- Sometimes, I agree with others even when I have a different opinion, just to avoid unnecessary tension.

#### Question 6
When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day to day life?

#### Answer
- I may speak loudly when someone repeatedly ignores my opinions.
- I become aggressive when I feel that someone is being unfair to me.
- Sometimes, I lose my patience when people do not understand my point.
- I sometimes react aggressively when someone blames me without understanding the situation.

#### Question 7
When do you switch into Passive Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day to day life?

#### Answer
- I sometimes use sarcasm when I feel upset but do not want to express my feelings directly.
- I may give silent treatment when I am angry with someone.
- Sometimes, I make indirect comments or taunts when someone hurts my feelings.
- I may complain about someone to others instead of talking to them directly.
- I sometimes hide my anger and show it through my behavior.
- I use passive-aggressive communication when I find it difficult to express my feelings openly.

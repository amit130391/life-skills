# Listening and Active Communication

## 1. Active Listening

#### Question 1
What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

#### Answer
Some of the steps/strategies to do Active Listening are:
1. Pay full attention to the speaker and avoid getting distracted by your own thoughts or other things around you.
2. Allow the other person to finish speaking completely before you respond or share your thoughts.
3. Use encouraging words and phrases like “I see” or “Tell me more” to show interest and encourage the speaker to continue.
4. Show that you are interested in the conversation through eye contact, nodding, and appropriate body language.
5. Write down important points during important conversations so that you can remember the information later.
6. Repeat or summarize what the speaker has said in your own words to make sure you have understood the message correctly.

## 2. Reflective Listening

#### Question 2
According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

#### Answer
According to Fisher's model, the key points of Reflective Listening are:
- Listen more and speak less, giving the speaker enough time to express their thoughts and feelings.
- Try to understand the speaker's feelings and personal experiences instead of focusing only on facts or general information.
- Repeat or explain what the speaker has said in your own words to show that you have understood their message.
- Avoid asking too many questions, giving advice, or telling the speaker what they should think or feel.
- Try to understand the situation from the speaker's point of view without judging them or comparing it with your own experiences.
- Show empathy, acceptance, and genuine interest so that the speaker feels comfortable sharing their thoughts and feelings.
